The player is now a real character model.

**New**
- The explorer. A textured, animated model made with Gemini and Meshy replaces the stand-in player. It idles, walks and runs.
- Messages sit about one third of the way down the screen, just above the character. Your eyes stay near the action.
- Footstep sounds are gone.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.2.1.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/

Tested in desktop Chrome with software graphics only. Villagers and animals are still stand-ins.
