# Keeper of the Hollow v0.15.0

The Hollow is night now, and every message, pop-up and menu looks like a page from a storybook. The keeper carries a lantern, and the night has new music. While Home Meadows gets its polish, it is the only land open: wider glades, new paths and warm lamp posts at every turn. Neighbors talk with their own portraits.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The title card is a parchment page with the name in red. Press Start.
2. Look around. It is night, the keeper holds a glowing lantern in the left hand and the night music is a new, softer track.
3. Walk up to Mira by the garden. A parchment tag shows the E key (or the Xbox X button, or Tap) and "Talk to Mira".
4. Talk to her. The speech box is parchment with her name on a red ribbon and her portrait in a round frame. The words type out with soft voice blips. Key words are green.
5. With an Xbox controller, press A while she talks: the whole line shows at once. Press A again: the box closes and the keeper does not jump.
6. Pick up a few finds. Parchment slips stack in the top left, three at most, newest on top.
7. Press Esc, Menu or View. A leather journal opens over the dimmed world. Up and down (or LB and RB) move through the list. The right page says what each entry holds.
8. Follow the Glade trail north from the Old Well to the Lamplight Glade. Lamp posts light the way, each with a soft pool of light on the ground. Sit on the bench there. The keeper sits on the seat, not inside it.
9. Walk to the cave mouth on Hollow Mountain and try to go in. A line says the dark is too deep tonight. Home Meadows first.
10. Settings: a new switch, Voice blips. Turn it off and talk to Mira again: no blips.

## New in this version

**The storybook look** (option A of three mockups, picked on 2026-11-15)
- Parchment panels with a brown edge for every message, pop-up and menu. Ink-brown text.
- Three new fonts: Fraunces for names and headings, Lora for speech, pop-ups and the journal, Nunito for the rest.
- The speech box: the speaker's name on a red ribbon, a round medallion with their first letter, key words in green, a small red arrow when the line is done.
- Text types out letter by letter. Reduce motion shows it at once.
- Voice blips while neighbors talk, a different pitch for each one. Settings > Voice blips turns them off.
- The prompt shows the button for the device you use: the E key, the Xbox X button or Tap.
- Pop-ups stack in the top left, three at most.
- The Menu opens a leather journal: the same entries in the same order on the left page, a short preview on the right.

**Night only**
- When the storm put out the Waylight, the sun went with it. The Hollow stays dark until the Waylight is lit again. Lamps, fireflies and the keeper's lantern are the only light.
- The keeper always carries a lit lantern in the left hand. The Lantern upgrade still makes its light reach further and lights the trail lamps.
- New night music: "Prairie nights" (CC0), in place of the made-in-code night track.

**Home Meadows, open and lit**
- Only Home Meadows is open while it gets its polish. The other lands wait, dark, until they are switched back on. The cave, the log tunnel and the mended bridge say so.
- The counter counts the 58 finds of Home Meadows. Finds you already have from other lands stay in your save.
- Four open areas with fewer trees: the Lamplight Glade, the Old Woods Clearing, the West Field and the South Green.
- Four new paths: the West lane, the Shore lane, the Glade trail and the Old Woods trail.
- Fifteen lamp posts at the crossings, glades and benches, each with a pool of warm light on the ground.
- Three new benches and three new signposts to fast travel to.
- While the Autumn Woods are closed, the shovel waits in the West Field and your second full set earns the Lantern.

**Portraits**
- Mira, Old Tom, Pip and Ada show a portrait drawn from their own model in the speech box. Elowen and the guardians keep their first letter for now.

**Fixes**
- Benches: the keeper sits on the middle of the seat instead of sinking into it.
- A on a controller moves the speech on (BC's ask).
