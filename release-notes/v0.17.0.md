# Keeper of the Hollow v0.17.0

New ways to move around Home Meadows. The keeper swims with real strokes, climbs ladders hand over hand to three high places, and glides on new cloth wings. Five secret spots hold notes from Anna, the first lamp keeper.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Start. Walk into the pond. The keeper swims with strokes and treads water when you stop.
2. Go to the West Field. A tall rock stands there with a ladder on its south side. Walk up to the ladder and press Act. The keeper climbs hand over hand to the top.
3. On top, look in the far corner for a small chest with a glint. Act on it: a note from Anna. Press Act at the top of the ladder to climb down.
4. Find the other two ladders: the Old Woods lookout, an old tree with a deck at the Old Woods Clearing, and the old watchtower near the South Green. Each top holds a secret.
5. Climb Hollow Mountain a little way, jump and hold Jump. The new glider opens: cream cloth wings with a red border.
6. Open the Journal. The extras line counts your secret spots out of five.

## New in this version

**Swimming**
- A swim stroke when you move and a slow paddle when you stop.

**Ladders and high places**
- Three ladder-only places in Home Meadows: the West Field rock, the Old Woods lookout and the old watchtower.
- Act at the foot climbs up. Act at the top climbs down. The keeper climbs hand over hand.
- Climbing a cliff with the Climbing Gloves uses the same climb.

**Secret spots**
- Five secret spots, each with a small chest and a note from Anna: one on top of each ladder place, one in a mushroom ring in the Lamplight Glade and one tucked under the bridge.
- The Journal counts them.

**The glider**
- New cloth wings in cream with a red border, dark veins, a wooden spar and two ribbons that trail behind.
