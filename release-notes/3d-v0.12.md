The game has a new name, Keeper of the Hollow, and a quest to go with it.

**The Waylight story**
- Seven new story cards open a new game. Skip leaves at any time. Menu > Story replays them.
- A great storm broke the Waylight, the lantern on Hollow Mountain, into ten shards. Princess Elowen calls for help up the Old Well. You are the small keeper who hears her.
- Six guardians hold the six shards in reach. Nobody fights. Each asks a favor or a riddle, and the answer is always something you find.
- The princess speaks through the Old Well: her first call, a line for each shard and a hint when nothing is new. She says it herself: take your time.
- This build stops at six shards. The other four wait for new lands.

**Anna's trail**
- Each set on display in the museum wakes one echo in the Old Well. Ten echoes bring the water back and your garden grows twice as fast.
- Neighbors share stories at 2 hearts, hand over keepsakes and point you to nine carved well stones and the first camp.
- A gold "!" shows over a neighbor with something new to say.

**A new look for text**
- Speech shows in a wide rounded see-through box low on the screen, with the speaker's name above it and key words in light blue.
- The Act prompt, one-line messages and the find counter use the same rounded look.
- One Menu button sits in the top right corner. Tap it to open Turn view, Map, Journal and Settings in a row. The old Menu list is now Settings.
- Messages leave 1 second sooner.

**New neighbors and real motions**
- Every person is a new KayKit character (CC0, from patch.gg): the explorer, all ten neighbors and the merchant.
- Neighbors wave when you walk up and cheer for a dish they love.
- You bend to pick things up, dig, swing the bug net, throw a stick for your dog and sit down at benches and campfires.
- Fishing has a full set of motions: cast, wait, bite and catch.

**Village and Christmas**
- The traveling merchant sets up the cart by the shore on even days and trades for home cooking.
- The notice board by the museum lists four bundles. Finish all four and the neighbors mend the Old Well: a new stone rim, a roof, a rope and a bucket.
- A Christmas theme switch on the title screen and in the Menu: ornaments, lights, wreaths, gift trees and light snow outdoors.

**Also new**
- A Full screen button in the browser build, on the title screen and in the Menu.
- A new app icon: the Waylight's lantern on a hilltop, ringed by its ten shards.
- The home page is rewritten for the new story, with new pictures.

**Good to know**
- Tested in desktop Chrome only, not yet on a phone. On a phone, check that the speech box stays clear of the touch buttons.
- The 2D game is unchanged and still shows its old name.
