Fixes from testing on the Retroid Pocket Flip 2. Every menu now fits every screen.

**Fixes**
- Menus center and scroll correctly on every Android version. Nothing is cut off at the top or bottom.
- Only one menu shows at a time. Menu backgrounds are solid, and the top buttons hide while a menu is open.
- On short screens held sideways, the pause menu uses two columns and the map sits beside fast travel.
- Walking speed is the same on screen in every direction. Your companion keeps up.
- Menus and the world scale up on tablets, laptops and TVs.
- On narrow phones, the treasure counter no longer covers the top buttons.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.13.1.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 11 screen sizes, upright and sideways. versionCode 21.
