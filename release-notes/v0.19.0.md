# Keeper of the Hollow v0.19.0

A new Lantern Hill start screen and the talk box from By Lantern's Glow. Talk now waits for you, tells you which button moves it on and never starts over by itself.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The start screen is a painted night: stars, a crescent moon, rolling hills, the Waylight tower glowing on the far hill and a lantern on the near hill with fireflies. The title glows softly.
2. Under the title: the controls line for what you used last, then Continue (your slot and finds), Play and Settings, and Version 0.19.0 at the bottom.
3. Press Continue. Walk to Mira and press X. Her box opens a third of the way down the screen, above your keeper, with her name on the red ribbon.
4. Wait. The box stays. When the words finish, a line reads Press A to close (or Press A for more on a long talk).
5. Press A until it closes. It stays closed. Press X again and she talks again.
6. Tap the box on your phone. It moves on the same way.

## New in this version

- The shared start screen (standard G5.21): Lantern Hill with the Waylight tower, the glowing title, one controls line, Continue only when the slot holds a game, then Play, Settings and Quit in the app. A new player starts with Play, which opens the slot list.
- The talk box follows the new talk standard (G5.20), the same as By Lantern's Glow:
  - It sits a third of the way down the screen and grows upward, so the text ends just above your keeper.
  - A talk stays until you close it. Short notes with no speaker, such as "Home. Your cabin and garden.", still leave by themselves.
  - When a page is written out, a line reads Press A for more or Press A to close, in the words of your device.
  - The press that closes a line never starts the talk again. Before, Act on a one-page line opened it once more.
  - A tap on the box finishes, turns or closes the page.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.10.0 (the new shared start screen and painted scenes).
