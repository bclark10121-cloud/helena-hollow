Small comforts for longer play.

**New**
- Button names match your controller. Xbox pads show A B X Y, PlayStation pads show the shapes and the Retroid shows Nintendo-style letters. Settings > Button labels lets you pick.
- Photo album. Menu > Album keeps your last 12 photos. Pick one to see it larger or remove it. The full photos stay in your gallery.
- Reduce motion. Settings > Reduce motion softens screen fades and flashes and turns off confetti and sparkle bursts.
- Each save slot on the title screen shows when it was last saved.
- A gentle backup tip after a few big milestones, only if you have not made a backup code in the last week.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.4.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways. Controller names were simulated. A real Xbox pad and the Retroid are the true test. versionCode 26. Targets Android 16.
