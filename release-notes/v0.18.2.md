# Keeper of the Hollow v0.18.2

Play with a controller on your phone and the touch buttons step out of the way. A new setting lets you choose when they show.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game on your phone and press Start. The Jump and Act buttons show.
2. Press any button on your controller. The touch buttons leave at once.
3. Tap the screen. They come back.
4. Open Settings > Touch buttons. Try Always (they stay even with a controller) and Off (they never show). Set it back to Auto.
5. Close the game, connect the controller first, then open it. No touch buttons at the start.

## New in this version

- The touch buttons hide the moment a controller or a key is used. Before, they stayed drawn on screen.
- A controller that is already connected, or gets connected, hides them too.
- Settings > Touch buttons: Auto, Always or Off.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1.

## Test checklist

- [ ] A controller press hides the touch buttons. A touch shows them again.
- [ ] Always and Off work, and the choice stays after a restart.
- [ ] The speech box sits low when the touch buttons are hidden.

## How it was tested

- On a cloud machine in headless Chrome, with a pretend touch screen and the kit's pretend Xbox controller: the v0.18 suite (four new touch button checks), the controller suite and the night life suite. All passed.
- Not tested yet: a real phone with a real controller, the Retroid.

## Install

Install `KeeperOfTheHollow-v0.18.2.apk` over the last build. Your saves and settings carry over.
