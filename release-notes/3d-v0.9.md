The climb gets its moment, and the Hollow gets a little history.

**New**
- A summit moment. The first time you stand on top of Hollow Mountain you find a logbook under the flag. Sign it.
- Treasure maps. Four riddles, each naming a place you know. A red X marks the spot once you hold the map. The shovel digs up a keepsake.
- Where maps come from: the summit logbook, Ada at the museum for your first and your third donated set, and the last message bottle.
- Neighbors on the move. Mira, Old Tom, Pip and Hazel walk to a second spot for the evening and are home by morning. They stop to talk when you come close.
- The Journal lists the riddles you hold and counts keepsakes.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.9.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/
