Five small things that make the Hollow easier to live in.

**New**
- Requests page. The last page of the journal lists who asked for what, where they live and how close you are. Ready ones sit at the top. The Old Well bundles are there too.
- Neighbors on the map. Blue crosses mark villagers. Gold ones have something waiting for you.
- Fast travel to the cabin door and the museum door, from anywhere.
- Name your companion on the Character screen. After a game of fetch it hops, spins or rolls in the grass, with a little heart.
- Phones with a camera cutout: the counter, buttons and menus now stay clear of it.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.2.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways. The cutout padding can only be proven on a real phone. versionCode 24. Targets Android 16.
