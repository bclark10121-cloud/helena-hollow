# Keeper of the Hollow v0.23.0

Photo mode gains filters, and the story chapters and photo mode now run on the shared kit. Everything else plays the same as 0.22.0.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.22.0. Your saves stay. Your album keeps the photos you already took.
2. Open photo mode (Menu, or press the right stick). Try Closer, Farther and Turn view.
3. Press Filter a few times: None, Warm, Cool, Black and white, Old photo. Take a photo with a filter on.
4. Press Album. Your old photos and the new one show, newest first.
5. Open the Journal. The chapter line reads the same as before.

## New

- Photo filters: Warm, Cool, Black and white and Old photo. The photo saves with the filter on.

## Under the hood

- Story chapters and the order neighbors share their news run on the kit story block (K4). The game's own flags stay where they were in the save.
- Photo mode runs on the kit photo block (K10): the button bar, the zoom, the filters and the ring of 12 photos per slot. The game keeps its camera, the Album page and the Wildlife Album.
- Photos from 0.22 move to the kit's file names the first time you open photo mode or the album.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.22.0 (was 1.17.1). Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Version 0.23.0 installs over 0.22.0 and keeps your saves and photos.

## Judgment calls

1. The kit photo bar keeps your Album button, placed just before Done, and uses the storybook button look.
2. The kit adds a small look-around pan. Keeper's camera does not use it yet. Turn view and the right stick still turn the view.
3. Deleting a slot removes its photos under both the old and the new names.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine:
- New `tests/kit_check.gd`: the kit chapters and neighbor news order match 0.22 for all 1,024 mixes of the story flags. Three 0.22 photos move into the kit album. Photo mode opens, zooms, picks a filter and closes (10 of 10 ok).
- The kit story and photo checks, MIGRATE, SAVESTORE, SETTINGS_SLOTS and DEBUGMENU checks.
- Browser tests: the controller smoke test (`web_pad`, all ok) and a new photo test (`web_v23`: P opens photo mode, a photo saves, the album opens, all ok). The old `web_v05` suite stalls on the slot list (it predates the v0.14 menus), so its photo checks moved to `web_v23`.
- The build check with the Tester tools gate. The APK carries the shared signing key.

Not tested: a real phone, and the long story suite (`web_v12`, 18 minutes). The kit check covers the story rules it would touch.
