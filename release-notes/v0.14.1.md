A quick fix release from playtesting.

**Fixes**
- Some treasures sat in pockets of trees where the only way in was a hidden one-tile lane. The Amber by the cave was the worst. Those spots now have a clear lane you can see and walk. Six treasures got room: Amber, Pinecone, Mushroom, Feather, Chestnut and the Bell.

**New look**
- The cave entrance in the meadows is now a rocky mountain with a snow cap and an arched cave mouth. Before, it looked like a stone building.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.1.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways. A new walk test moves like a player with a controller, with no teleporting, and reaches all 99 placed treasures. versionCode 23. Targets Android 16.
