The Hollow has neighbors with fur and feathers.

**New**
- Wildlife Album. 14 animals live around the Hollow: a rabbit, squirrel, frog, heron, gull, deer, fox, owl, snow hare, ibex, lizard, roadrunner, cave bat and a glowing salamander. Open Photo mode and take their picture to add them to the album. They wander near home and step away if you rush up. The owl only comes out at night.
- The Wildlife Album is a new set with its own journal page and a new case in the museum. There are now 148 things to find.
- Stamp card. A journal page of 18 friendly stamps, like First Find, Pond Angler, Night Owl and Best Friend. No timers and no pressure. Each one tells you how to earn it.
- Calendar touches. In October, leaves fall over the title screen and pumpkins glow at the bottom. In December, it snows.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.5.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways, plus a new-game audit that confirms every animal can be reached. versionCode 27. Targets Android 16.
