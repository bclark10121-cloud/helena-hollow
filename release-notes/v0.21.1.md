# Keeper of the Hollow v0.21.1

The owl no longer hoots at night. Frogs by the pond still croak.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.21.0. Your saves stay.
2. Tap Play and your slot.
3. Wait for night in Home Meadows, or use Tester tools > Time > Night.
4. Walk around for two or three minutes. No owl "hoo hoo".
5. Walk to the pond. The frogs still croak.
6. Open Settings > Ambience. It still sets the frogs, cave drips and wind.

## New in this version

- The owl hoot from the trees is gone (asked for by BC on 2026-11-16).
- Frogs stay. Grandmother Owl, the owl you photograph and the carved owl stay. None of them make a sound.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0, no change.

## Install

Version 0.21.1 installs over 0.21.0 and keeps the saves in slots 1 to 5.
