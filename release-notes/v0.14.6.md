A small fix from playtesting.

**Fixes**
- Every message now opens at the top of the screen, under the buttons. Before, villager speech and longer messages opened lower down while find messages opened at the top.
- When a find message and a longer message show together, the find message sits right below.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.6.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways. versionCode 28. Targets Android 16.
