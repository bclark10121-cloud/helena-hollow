The 3D game grows from a first slice into the whole Hollow.

**New**
- Arms. The player and every villager have arms that swing and wave.
- Music and sound. Six gentle loops: meadow, night, cave, snow, canyon and indoors. Menu has Music and Sound volume.
- Tools. Pick up the fishing rod, bug net and shovel. Dig mounds, catch bugs and fish in the pond, the sea, Mirror Pond and the underground lake.
- Requests. All nine villagers ask for something and give a gift. Their gifts open the garden, the mountain gate, the boat and the rope ladder.
- The bridge is broken at first. Find 8 treasures and it mends itself.
- New places: the cave, the Deep Cave, the Snowy Peaks, Sunny Canyon, your cabin and the museum hall.
- Museum. Walk up to a case with a complete set to put it on display.
- Garden. Plant Mira's seeds beside the cabin.
- Wildlife. Walk near an animal and press Act to take its photo.
- Day and night. The bed in your cabin switches between them. Rain comes and goes.
- Stamps and upgrades in the journal.
- One-line messages sit a little lower. Long text stays at the top.

**Not in yet**
- The pet and its 5 Pet Finds, festivals, cooking, the old well, cabin decorating and the map.
- Final art. Models are still simple stand-ins.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.2.apk` below. It installs over 3D v0.1 and keeps that save. It sits next to the 2D game and does not touch its save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/

Tested in desktop Chrome with software graphics only. Speed on real devices is unknown. The music was not checked by ear.
