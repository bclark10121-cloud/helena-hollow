A friendlier Hollow, built from what players love and dislike in Stardew Valley and A Short Hike.

**New**
- Free look. Turn the view smoothly with the right stick, Z and C, or a drag on the right side of the screen.
- Menu > Auto camera. Switch it off for one steady view everywhere.
- Scenery close to the camera thins out, so a tree never hides you.
- Trail guide. The Journal opens with one gentle next step. The Map shows finds left in each land.
- A bounce cap grows beside the cabin from the first minute.
- Neighbors have more to say. Lines change by day, night and rain, and each neighbor shares a short scene at 3 hearts.
- Lost items. Mira, Old Tom and Hazel have each lost something. Find it and bring it back.
- The pond lap. Six ribbons round the pond, in order, with no clock.
- A beach ball on the south shore. Keep it in the air and beat your best rally.
- Festivals. Ring the bell by the museum to start one. Four festivals, five lanterns to find in each, no dates and no deadlines.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.8.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/
