A mountain to hike, cliffs to climb and water that falls.

**New**
- Hollow Mountain. A tall peak north of the cabin. One trail winds round it five times to the summit.
- Climbing Gloves wait in a chest at the top. With them you climb any cliff outdoors. There is no stamina bar.
- Cairns. Four stone stacks sit on cliff tops in the Snowy Peaks and Sunny Canyon. Climb up and add your stone.
- High lakes, streams and waterfalls.
- A rope bridge in the Autumn Woods and wooden steps on steep trails.
- Benches at lookouts and campfires to rest at.
- The camera pulls back on the mountain and at lookouts to show the view.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.4.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/

Tested in desktop Chrome with software graphics only.
