You are no longer alone on the trail.

**New**
- A companion. After 12 finds a little orange cat joins you. It follows you everywhere.
- Fetch. With nothing nearby, press the action button to throw a stick. Fetch brings back the 5 Pet Finds.
- Name your companion in the Menu.
- Friendship hearts. Chat with a neighbor once a day and finish their request. Letters arrive in your mailbox.
- Photo mode and a photo album. Press P or open the Menu.
- Pin a find. Tap any ??? in the journal and the map shows where to look.
- Comfort settings: Reduce motion and Large text. Button names follow your controller, keyboard or touch.
- Ambient sound: birds by day, crickets at night, wind on the peaks and drips in the caves.
- Finds tucked behind trees stay hidden until you walk close.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.5.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/

Tested in desktop Chrome with software graphics only.
