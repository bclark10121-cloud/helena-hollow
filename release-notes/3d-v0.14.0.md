# Keeper of the Hollow 3D v0.14.0

The game moves onto the shared standards every game follows: one Settings menu, five save slots, a standard Menu row and Tester tools. No new content in this release.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The title card reads "A cozy quest   Version 0.14.0" and shows Continue, Play and Settings. Continue opens the slot you played last.
2. Menu > Settings. Turn on Full screen in the browser. Change Music and Text size. Close the game, open it again: your changes are kept.
3. Open the Menu row. It reads Resume, Settings, How to play, Map, Journal, Turn view, More, Title. The world stops while it is open.
4. Switch to another tab and come back. The Menu row is open and nothing moved.
5. Title > Play. Five slots. Delete a slot you do not need: the first press asks, the second deletes.
6. With an Xbox controller only: get from the title card into the game, open Settings with View, move with the D-pad, close with B.

## New in this version

**Settings, the same in every game**
- Music and Sound in five steps (Off, Low, Medium, High, Full), Full screen in the browser, Frame rate, and Rumble on a phone or with a controller.
- The game's own: Camera, Auto camera, Picture, Auto pickup, Christmas theme and Controls.
- Text size (Normal, Large, Larger), Reduce motion and Reset settings.
- Every change works at once and saves by itself. Your old settings carry over.
- The same Settings opens from the title card and from the Menu row.

**Save slots**
- Five slots. Slots 1 to 5 from v0.13 carry over. Slots 6 to 10 are gone.
- The title card: Continue opens the slot you played last, Play opens the slot list.
- Each slot shows its finds, the day and when you played it. Delete needs a second press.

**Menu row and pause**
- The row reads Resume, Settings, How to play, Map, Journal, Turn view, More, Title.
- The world pauses while the row or any page is open, when you switch tabs or apps and when your controller turns off.
- How to play lists every key, every Xbox button and every touch control.
- More holds the story, photo mode and album, your companion, backup codes, Back to the cabin and Start over. Start over and Import each need a second press.

**Also**
- Tester tools behind a code in Settings, with Copy bug report (test builds only).
- A new app icon set from the lantern painting, with a white lantern for Android themed icons.
- Saves and backup codes are read and written more safely, with a backup copy of each slot.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1.

## Install

Install `KeeperOfTheHollow-v0.14.0.apk` over the last 3D build. Slots 1 to 5 carry over. Saves in slots 6 to 10 are not read anymore.

## Full test checklist

The full checklist is in the roadmap under "New in 3D v0.14.0". Run in the cloud browser tests:
- The controller smoke test: title card, story cards, the Menu row in order, Map, Journal, How to play, Settings, walking, jumping and talking with only a pretend Xbox controller.
- The standards test: Start and Continue, the slot list, a new game in slot 2, Delete on a second press, Settings kept across a reload, Settings fits a phone, a tablet and a desktop, Tester tools asks for the code, Start over on a second press, pause on focus loss.
- The headless checks: save store, settings and slots, debug menu, the move of old slots and settings, reach and access reports.
- Test hooks off for players: only the whole-word `?test` flag turns them on (6 page addresses, `errors []`).
- Tools, regions, the museum, day and night and saving: a play-through from the first find to a donated set, with the save kept across a reload (34 checks, `errors []`).
- The signed APK: version 0.14.0, version code 18, no internet permission, Tester tools behind the code.
- After the kit 1.9.1 pull: the standards test again (23 checks) and the controller smoke test, both `errors []`.
