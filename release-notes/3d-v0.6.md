New scenery, a better glider and room for more saves.

**New**
- 10 save slots on the title screen. Each used slot has an X to delete it. Tap twice to confirm.
- Delete all saves, also on the title screen. Settings stay.
- Menu > Save now.
- New scenery from Meshy models: pine, leafy and autumn trees, mossy boulders, berry bushes, wooden signposts, a log cabin and a stone museum.
- Dive and swoop. While gliding, let go of jump to dive. Hold it again and the fall turns into speed and a short climb.
- Music follows the land. Bells join the tune as you climb. At a bench or a cairn the tune steps back for the birds and the wind.

**Fixed**
- A save made by an older build could load inside a hill or under the ground. Those saves now start on open ground or at the cabin.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.6.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/
