Every treasure has a new icon.

**New look**
- All 134 treasures are redrawn as clean, shaded icons with a dark outline. Before, they were tiny pixel drawings blown up and smoothed, so many turned into blobs. Now a chestnut looks like a chestnut and a carp looks like a fish.
- The new icons show in the world, the journal, the museum and when you find something.
- The pixel view keeps the original pixel art.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.3.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways. Every icon was checked by eye on a contact sheet. versionCode 25. Targets Android 16.
