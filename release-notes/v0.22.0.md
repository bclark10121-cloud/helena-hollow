# Keeper of the Hollow v0.22.0

The opening story now plays in the storybook speech box, as you picked in the playtest sweep. The game moves to Godot 4.7.2, draws at your screen's own rate (up to 120 frames a second on your phone), opens full screen in the browser on the first tap and fills wide screens edge to edge.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.21.2. Your saves stay.
2. Start a new game in an empty slot. The story of the Waylight plays in the parchment speech box over the Hollow, one card a page. Press A (or E, or tap) for more. The welcome line follows the last page.
3. Menu > More > Story plays it again the same way.
4. On your 120 Hz phone, Settings > Tester tools > Show frame rate (or Settings > Frame rate). It reads up to 120 while you walk.
5. In a browser, the first tap turns full screen on. Turning Full screen off in Settings is remembered.

## New in this version

- The opening story in the speech box (BC, 2026-11-19 sweep: "Yes, move them"). The full-screen story cards and their drawn pictures are gone. The text is unchanged.
- Smooth at up to 120 frames a second (G6.20): no 60 cap, 120 physics steps a second, so the keeper moves on every frame of a 120 Hz screen. Walking speed is the same at 60 and 120. Show frame rate in Tester tools.
- Full screen by default in the browser (G5.11, kit 1.17).
- 1080p and wider screens fill edge to edge (G6.21).

## Changed

- Godot 4.7.2 (G6.19).
- Kit 1.17.1. The kit no longer caps phones and the browser at 60 frames a second (G6.20).

## Install

Version 0.22.0 installs over 0.21.2 and keeps your saves.

## Test checklist

- [ ] The opening story reads well in the speech box, one card a page.
- [ ] Walking and the camera feel smooth on your phone. The frame rate reads up to 120.
- [ ] Battery saver still caps at 30.
- [ ] The Retroid still plays smoothly (120 physics steps cost more).

## Judgment calls

1. The story cards' small drawn pictures are gone with the full-screen panel. The speech box shows the Hollow behind the text instead.
2. 120 physics steps a second (one of the two ways G6.20 allows) instead of physics smoothing. It is the safer change for this game. On a 90 Hz screen the steps alternate one and two per frame. Physics smoothing would even that out, as a later step.
3. The opening story marks itself as seen when it starts, so leaving it early never replays it. Menu > More > Story plays it again.
4. Kit 1.17.1 removes the kit's 60 frame cap for every game at its next kit pull. The old E5.2 line asked for a 60 cap on phones. G6.20 replaces it. Listed for you to confirm.
