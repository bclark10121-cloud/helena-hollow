The camera comes in close, like a toy diorama on a table.

**Closer camera**
- A new default camera, Close and low: about 8 m back outdoors, a flatter angle and a wider lens.
- Settings > Camera steps through four styles: Classic (the view up to v0.12), Closer, Diorama and Close and low. Your pick is kept.
- Name labels shrink as the camera comes closer, so names no longer fill the screen.
- The summit, benches and cairns still pull the camera back to show the view.

**Menu and keys**
- Esc opens the row by the Menu button and folds it away. It closes any open screen first.
- The phone's Back does the same.
- M opens the Map.

**Xbox controller**
- Play the whole game with only an Xbox controller. A chooses the highlighted button and B goes back in every menu.
- View and Menu both open the Menu row.
- The help text in Settings and the game page use Xbox button names. The game page shows how to connect a controller.

**Small fixes**
- A neighbor who is walking when you arrive now waves once they stop.
- Talk and pick-up prefer a person or a thing over a nearby animal. An animal no longer takes the Act press from the summit logbook.
- The title screen reads "A cozy quest", like the home page.

**Test checklist**
- [ ] A fresh install starts on Close and low. Settings > Camera brings back Classic.
- [ ] Close the game and open it again. The camera pick is kept.
- [ ] Glide down from Hollow Mountain and climb a cliff. You can still see where you are going.
- [ ] Esc, View, Menu and phone Back open and fold the Menu row.
- [ ] With only an Xbox controller: start a slot, skip the story, open the Map, Journal and Settings, walk, jump and talk.
- [ ] Walk up to a neighbor on their evening walk. They wave once they stop.
- [ ] On a phone, upright and sideways, the Act prompt sits above the character.

**Good to know**
- Tested in headless Chrome on a desktop, not on a phone or the Retroid.
- Every player starts on Close and low, also when updating from v0.12.
- Built with the shared kit 1.5.0 (G10.4).
- Test tools in the browser build now switch on only for test runs. Players never see them.
- The touch buttons stay on screen while you use a controller.

Download `KeeperOfTheHollow-v0.13.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/keeper-of-the-hollow/
