# Keeper of the Hollow v0.18.0

Your cabin gets real furniture, Home Meadows looks lived in and each part of the meadow has its own song. Every long talk now shows in the storybook speech box, page by page. The hollow logs are solid, and there are five new camera styles to try.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Start. Walk into your cabin. Look for the bed, the couch corner, the picture frames and the new table, chair, lamp and bookcase.
2. Walk out to the Pond King by the pond and talk to him. His ask shows in the speech box with his portrait. Press Act to turn the page.
3. Read the notice board by the museum. It reads out in the speech box with a Close button.
4. Walk from the Lamplight Glade to the Old Woods and on to the South Green. The music changes with each area.
5. Open Settings. Try Camera (nine styles) and Music (By area, or one song everywhere).
6. Walk into a hollow log. You stop at it.

## New in this version

**The cabin**
- KayKit furniture for the table, chair, bookcase, lamp, rug, potted plant, driftwood shelf and pumpkin stool.
- A bed, a couch corner with a low table and three picture frames.

**Home Meadows**
- Barrels, crates, sacks, a wheelbarrow, a bucket and pumpkins by the cabin, the garden, the Old Well, the museum, the shore cart, the hen yard and the bridge.

**Music**
- A song for each area: the Lamplight Glade, the Old Woods, the West Field and the South Green. The night song plays between them.
- Walk back into an area and its song picks up where it left off.
- Settings > Music: By area, or one song everywhere.

**Storybook speech everywhere**
- The Pond King, Elowen's first call, Ada's truth, Anna's camp, the last echo and Anna's book now show in the speech box, page by page, like any neighbor.
- The notice board reads out in the speech box. Turn in a ready bundle with its button.

**Fixes and options**
- The hollow logs are solid. Act at the mouth is the only way through, once the shortcut opens.
- Five new camera styles: Over the shoulder, Far and wide, Top down, Toy box and Storybook.
