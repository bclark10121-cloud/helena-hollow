Three tools learn a second trick.

**New**
- Trail lamps. Six old lamps stand along the trails. Touch your Lantern to one and it stays lit for good.
- Seed puffs. Six of them drift near the paths. Catch them with the bug net and flowers bloom by the cabin door.
- Bramble thickets. Three of them hide a small carved bird. The Climbing Gloves let you reach in.
- Menu > Picture. Sharp, Soft, or Softer and faster. The softer settings draw the 3D view with fewer pixels, which helps slower devices.
- The Journal counts lamps, seed puffs and carved birds.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.10.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/
