The Hollow is no longer flat.

**New land**
- Real hills across the Home Meadows and the Autumn Woods. The land climbs as it leaves the water. Trails stay gentle.
- The Snowy Peaks are a true mountain. Follow the trail up to the lookout, then glide back down.
- Sunny Canyon has tall mesas.
- Updrafts. Glide into a column of rising streaks and hold Jump. It carries you up.
- The camera looks over hills that get in the way.
- Nothing is out of reach. The game checks every find, neighbor and door each time it builds the world.

**New screens**
- A title screen with 3 save slots. Your old save is in slot 1.
- A map with fast travel to any signpost you have walked near. Press the Map button or M.
- Backup codes. Menu > Copy backup code.
- The journal shows a hint for each unfinished set and a list of requests.
- The frame rate readout shows the lowest rate of the last 10 seconds.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.3.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/

Tested in desktop Chrome with software graphics only.
