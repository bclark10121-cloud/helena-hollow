Deep Cave. A new level below the cave, plus fixes from playtesting.

**New**
- The Deep Cave: an underground lake, an old mine and glowing finds.
- Bram the miner stands by a shaft in the cave. Show him three Cave Crystals and he lowers his rope ladder.
- 14 new treasures in 3 new sets: Deep Glow, Old Mine and Lake Fish. 129 treasures in all.
- Fishing in the underground lake.
- A Miner's Lantern for the cabin when you finish the Old Mine set.

**Character**
- A front view with both eyes when walking down, and a back view when walking up.
- Each leg lifts in turn when walking up or down. Left and right keep the side stride.
- The Character screen preview shows all three views.

**Fixes**
- The controller can reach Random everything and Random look.
- The player stays visible in front of the museum walls.
- Bigger text in menus, dialogs and toasts.
- A Full screen button in the browser version. F also toggles it.
- Fixed a frozen first frame on slow loads.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.13.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome. versionCode 20.
