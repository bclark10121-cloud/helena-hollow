The water is open.

**New**
- Swimming. Walk into the pond, a lake, the river or the sea and you float. No breath meter and no way to sink. Jump gives a small hop.
- Message bottles. Five bottles drift far from shore, each with a note from the first keeper of the Hollow. Swim out to one, or cast the fishing rod near it to reel it in.
- Bounce caps. Three dry sprouts wait beside ledges. Loosen the soil with the shovel and a sprout grows into a cap that throws you high.
- The Journal counts bottles and bounce caps and keeps the notes you have read.

**Changed**
- Deep water no longer puts you back on the shore.
- The river still cannot be crossed until the bridge is mended.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.7.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/
