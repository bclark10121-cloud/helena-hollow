# Keeper of the Hollow v0.18.1

The steady cricket hum that played under the night is gone. Background sounds now have their own volume in Settings.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Start. Open Settings and set Music to Off.
2. Walk around Home Meadows at night. You hear your steps, and now and then an owl or a frog. No steady hum or hiss.
3. Open Settings and set Ambience to Off. The owls and frogs go quiet. Footsteps stay.
4. Set Ambience back to Full.

## New in this version

- The made-in-code night cricket bed no longer plays.
- Settings > Ambience (Off, Low, Medium, High, Full) sets the owls, frogs, cave drips, wind and birdsong apart from Sound.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.9.1.
