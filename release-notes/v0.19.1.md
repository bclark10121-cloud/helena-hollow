# Keeper of the Hollow v0.19.1

A start screen of its own. Lantern Hill stays with Pixel Hollow (BC, 2026-11-17).

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The start screen shows the Waylight tower on a rocky island at night. Its beam swings across the sea, the six shards circle its light and the storm clouds part around the moon.
2. The buttons stay their own width under the long title. Play and Settings work as in v0.19.0.
3. Turn on Reduce motion in Settings and go back to the title. The scene holds still.

## New in this version

- The Waylight start screen, drawn in code (`scripts/title_art.gd`).
- Kit 1.10.0 update: the title stays on one line and the buttons keep their width.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.10.0.

## How it was tested

- On a cloud machine in headless Chrome with the kit's pretend Xbox controller: the v0.19 suite (start screen and talk box, 9 checks) passed. The screenshot was checked by eye.
- Not tested yet: a real phone, the Retroid and a real controller.

## Install

Version 0.19.1 installs over 0.19.0 and keeps your saves.
