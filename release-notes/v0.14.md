Slow down and look around. Five new ways to enjoy the Hollow, plus a full game review.

**New**
- Photo mode. Open it from the pause menu. Look around, zoom, try filters, hide your character and add a label. Photos save to Pictures/Helena Hollow on Android and download in the browser.
- Journal hints. Pick a missing treasure to see which area it is in, the nearest signpost and any tool it needs. Pick it again to pin it on the map.
- Pet fetch. Press the action button with nothing nearby to throw a ball. Your companion brings it back, and sometimes one of 5 new Pet Finds.
- Background sounds. Birds, waves, wind, cave drips, the underground lake, a desert hawk and the cabin stove. Set the level in Settings > Ambience.
- Walk-in museum. Step through the door into a hall with a glass case for every set. Ada the curator takes donations and has a note for each exhibit.

**Fixes**
- Set rewards like the Lantern now arrive the moment you finish a set. Before, closing the game in that instant could lose them. Saves that missed them get them back.
- The cabin stove and workbench are solid.
- Back gestures on newer Android open the pause menu.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways, plus a new-game playthrough audit and a random input test. versionCode 22. Targets Android 16.
