The first look at Helena Hollow in 3D, built in Godot. This is an early slice, not the full game.

**What is in it**
- The Home Meadows and the Autumn Woods as real 3D ground with hills.
- Walk, jump and glide. Hold Jump in the air to glide. There is no stamina and nothing can hurt you.
- A mountain to climb in the north, with a flag on top. Glide off the summit.
- 57 treasures to pick up, villagers who say hello, a journal and a save.
- Menu > Import 2D save code brings your finds over from the 2D game.

**What is not in it yet**
- Tools, requests, fishing, the caves, the Snowy Peaks, Sunny Canyon, wildlife and stamps.
- Final art. The trees, houses and people are simple stand-ins.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.1.apk` below. It installs next to the 2D game and does not touch its save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/

Tested only in desktop Chrome with software graphics. Speed on real devices is unknown.
