# Keeper of the Hollow v0.20.0

Release 7: the standards review pieces every game now shares. Records with your 18 stamps and your stats, button remapping, backup codes in the slot list, Battery saver and music at one steady level.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.19.1, or open the game page and press Play in your browser. Your saves stay. The start screen shows Version 0.20.0.
2. Press Continue and walk around Home Meadows for a minute.
3. Open the Menu. Records sits after More. Press it. You see the 18 stamps, then your stats: finds, sets, stamps, hearts, days, photos and play time. Stamps you already had show as Earned.
4. Earn a stamp, for example find something new at night. The game says "Stamp earned" as before, and Records shows it with today's date.
5. Open Settings, then Change controls. Pick Act and press another key or button. If another action had it, the two swap. Act with the new one. Reset controls puts everything back.
6. In Settings, turn on Battery saver, look around, then turn it off.
7. Open the slot list from Play. Press Copy code on your slot. Paste it on another device and press Load code.
8. Listen to the music in the glade, the Old Woods, the West Field, the South Green and indoors. It should sound about as loud as before. Indoors is a little louder now, level with outdoors.

## New in this version

- Records in the Menu: your 18 stamps as achievements and 7 stats on one page (G4.10, G4.11). A stamp and its achievement are earned at the same moment.
- Change controls in Settings for walking, jump and glide, Act, Journal, Map, turning and looking, and photo mode (G5.22).
- Battery saver in Settings (G6.12). Keeper keeps its own Picture setting.
- Backup codes in the slot list: Copy code and Load code (G5.19). The HH3 codes in More still work.
- Old saves are now tested every release (G5.24).
- An error log on the device. Copy bug report in Tester tools adds the last ten errors (G5.27).
- Every song, sound and effect at one loudness level (G5.28). The game plays music louder by the same amount, so it sounds the same. Effects that touched the top of the range are a touch quieter.
- Back sits at the top left of Settings, the slot list, Records and Change controls (G5.30). The journal menu, map and More get it in the next standards release.
