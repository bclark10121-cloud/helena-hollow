# Keeper of the Hollow v0.21.0

Play holding your phone upright or sideways. Back now sits at the top left of every panel. The start screen plays its own title track, "Dreams Become Real".

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.20.0. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game upright. The title track starts with the start screen, no tap needed. The screen fits the tall phone.
3. Tap Settings. Back sits at the top left. Tap Back.
4. Tap Play and your slot. The title track fades into the meadow's music.
5. Walk around upright for a minute. The keeper and the path ahead stay in view.
6. Tap Menu. Upright, the book stacks its list over its preview page.
7. Open the Journal, the Map and More. Each has Back at the top left.
8. Turn the phone sideways mid-play. Play keeps going and the layout switches.

## New in this version

- Full play upright and sideways, on Android and in the browser. The game turns with your phone.
- Upright: menus and text sized for a tall screen, a camera lens set for the narrow view and the Menu book's pages stacked.
- Back at the top left of the Journal, the Map, More, How to play, the photo album and the pick lists. Close at the top right is gone.
- Start screen music: "Dreams Become Real" by Kevin MacLeod, picked by BC on 2026-11-17. On Android it starts with the start screen. In a browser it starts on the first tap.
- Turning the phone mid-play keeps your game. Nothing reloads.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0: the shared header bar with Back at the top and the screen fit for both orientations.

## Install

Version 0.21.0 installs over 0.20.0 and keeps the saves in slots 1 to 5. The game follows your phone's rotation lock. Turn the lock off to play upright.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: the new five-size browser check with a turn mid-play, the controller, menu and review browser checks, the reach report and the save, Settings, Tester tools, migration and old save checks. The title track was checked on the start screen and fading into play. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.
