The cabin becomes a home, and the playtest notes are in.

**Playtest fixes**
- Sand is no longer white. A drawing fault washed out bright ground on phones. It is fixed.
- Moodier light: a lower, warmer sun, cooler shade and a soft dark edge around the picture.
- The Act prompt is large and sits in a box. Names over neighbors and signs are bigger.
- Messages leave by themselves. A short line stays 3 seconds. Long notes stay a little longer. No more tap to close.
- Menu > Auto pickup. Walk up to a find and it is yours. Photos still need Act.
- Wooden steps show only where a trail really climbs.

**New**
- The hen, the goat and the duck like a visit. After three days the hen shares eggs and the goat shares milk.
- A stove in the cabin with 8 recipes. Neighbors teach some of them as you make friends.
- Share a dish with a neighbor once a day.
- A workbench that builds 3 pieces from things you have found.
- 8 furniture spots in the cabin and 22 pieces to earn and place.
- Water the garden to help it along.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow3D-v0.11.apk` below. It installs over earlier 3D builds and keeps the save. Or play in the browser: https://bclark10121-cloud.github.io/helena-hollow/3d/
