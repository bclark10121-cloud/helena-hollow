The neighbors get their real faces, and fishing finally looks like fishing.

**Neighbors**
- All 10 neighbors are now the Meshy models: Mira, Old Tom, Pip, Juniper, Captain Wren, Hazel, Frost, Sol, Bram and Ada.
- They breathe in place when standing and walk properly on their evening routes.
- They turn to face you when you come close.

**Fishing**
- The explorer holds a real fishing rod with a line to the bobber.
- A new cast motion: wind up over the shoulder, then cast. The bobber flies out in an arc into open water.
- He holds the rod while waiting. On a bite the bobber dips and a new catch motion lifts it in.
- Walk off while waiting and the line reels in.

**Good to know**
- The download is bigger: about 55 MB for the APK and about 30 MB for the browser build.
