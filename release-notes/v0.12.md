Village Life. Stardew-style village features for the hollow.

**New**
- Friendship hearts with every villager. Chat each day and share home-cooked dishes.
- A seasonal festival at the notice board by the museum, every season.
- Cooking at the cabin stove. Recipes arrive by mail as friendships grow.
- Four village bundles. Finish them to restore the Old Well and grow crops twice as fast.
- A hen, a duck and a goat to visit. The hen and goat share eggs and milk for cooking.
- Workbench crafting and a traveling merchant who trades for dishes.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.12.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

Tested in desktop Chrome. versionCode 19.
