A Christmas theme you can switch on any time.

**New**
- Christmas theme. Turn it on from the title screen or in Settings > Christmas theme.
- Every tree gets ornaments. Pines get a gold star. Palms and cacti join in too.
- The cabin and the museum get strings of lights and a wreath. The cave mouth gets a wreath. Signposts get a red bow.
- Little gift trees with presents stand in every area, including the caves, the cabin and the museum hall.
- Light snow falls outdoors and on the title screen.
- It is off by default and changes nothing about your save.

**Changed**
- One-line messages like "Found: Daisy!" sit a little lower, clear of the buttons. Speech and long text stay at the top.

**Install**
Download `HelenaHollow-v0.14.7.apk` below and open it on your Android device. It installs over earlier versions and keeps your save.

The decorations show in the angled view. The pixel view gets the snow only. Tested in desktop Chrome at 12 screen sizes, upright and sideways. versionCode 29. Targets Android 16.
