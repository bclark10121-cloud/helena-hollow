# Keeper of the Hollow v0.16.0

Home Meadows comes alive at night. Fireflies drift through the glades, moths dance at the lamps, rings spread across the pond, owls call from the trees and frogs croak at the water's edge. New music, a page for every find, and the princess now answers your questions.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Start. The night music is a new soft piano track, "First Light Particles".
2. Walk to the pond. Rings spread on the water. Stand still a few seconds: frogs croak near you, and now and then an owl hoots from the trees.
3. Follow the Glade trail north to the Lamplight Glade. Fireflies drift over the grass. Small moths flutter around each lamp post.
4. Pick up a find. A parchment card shows at the top: its picture, its name, its line of text and how far along its set is. It leaves by itself.
5. Go to the Old Well by Mira's garden and talk to Elowen. Her portrait shows in the speech box. Three buttons sit under her line: Tell me about the Waylight, Tell me about Anna and Goodbye. Pick one. B or Esc closes them.
6. Walk into the cabin. The indoor music is a new gentle piano tune, "Peaceful 3".
7. On a phone: the Jump and Act buttons are parchment discs with a brown edge. Press a key or use a controller and they hide. Touch the screen and they come back.

## New in this version

**Night life in Home Meadows**
- Fireflies in the four glades, by the pond and on the South Green.
- Moths at all fifteen lamp posts.
- Rings spread across the pond.
- An owl hoots from the trees now and then. Frogs croak when you are near the pond. Both follow the Sound volume.
- The Old Well glows a faint blue, the one lit landmark in the meadow.

**Music**
- Night: "First Light Particles" by Yoiyami (CC0), in place of "Prairie nights". As you climb Hollow Mountain, a soft high version of the same piece fades in.
- Cabin and museum: "Peaceful 3" by Tozan (CC0), in place of the music made in code.

**The storybook look, part 2**
- A find card for every new find, with the set's progress.
- Buttons are warm parchment pills.
- Choice buttons under a line of speech. Elowen at the Old Well is the first to use them.
- Portraits for Elowen and the Pond King.
- New touch buttons in the storybook look, shown only while you play by touch.

## Not in this version yet
