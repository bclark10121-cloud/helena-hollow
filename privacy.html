Privacy policy

Helena Hollow for Android and the browser version on this site. Effective November 13, 2026.
Short version. Helena Hollow collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The Android app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What the game stores

  • Your save files and settings stay on your device, in the app's own storage. They never leave the device unless you choose to export them.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes them. Android's own device backup may keep a copy if you turned that on in your phone settings. That backup is handled by Google under your account, not by the game.

Things you choose to do

  • Backup codes. The game turns your save into a text code. You decide where it goes: the clipboard or the Android share menu. The game does not send it anywhere on its own.
  • Photo mode. When you take a photo, the game saves a picture of the game screen to your device's Pictures/Helena Hollow folder. Nothing else is in the picture. It is never uploaded.
  • Vibration. The game asks Android to vibrate for small moments of feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

The browser version

  • The browser version stores saves in your browser's local storage on your own computer or phone.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request data, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of this, this page will say so before that version ships, and the change will appear in the release notes.

Contact

Questions go to the developer through the Helena Hollow issue page or the contact email on the Google Play listing.
